# Design Direction: Signal Ledger

## Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Signal Ledger

**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast editorial security report that treats each score as an evidence entry in a forensic ledger. Warm paper, ink black, signal orange, and acid chartreuse create a human, serious, non-generic technical tone.

**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Quiet Observatory

**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, dark observability environment with restrained blue-green telemetry accents and generous space for long-form analysis.

**Probability:** 0.04

## Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Campus Systems Map

**Very Brief Intro:** A bright, diagram-led academic poster system that turns the audit into a visual map of modules, dependencies, and gaps.

**Probability:** 0.02

## Chosen Approach: Signal Ledger

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial data journalism with forensic lab-notebook references.

**Core Principles:** Evidence before decoration; asymmetry over generic centered dashboards; contrast between blunt critique and approachable guidance; every interactive element must expose more context rather than merely animate.

**Color Philosophy:** Ink black carries seriousness and focus. Warm ivory makes the report feel reviewable and human rather than terminal-like. Signal orange marks risk and friction. Acid chartreuse marks the few places where the project genuinely works or where progress is possible.

**Layout Paradigm:** A persistent left rail behaves like a report index while the main canvas alternates between a large score statement, asymmetric evidence cards, and full-width visual exhibits. The layout intentionally avoids a centered SaaS dashboard template.

**Signature Elements:** Redacted-style evidence labels; thin measurement rules with orange annotation ticks; a recurring broken-hexagon mark that represents a promising system whose production loop is incomplete.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions reveal the reasoning behind the score. Tabs filter evidence, chart points expose category notes, and the share/save controls preserve the current report state without pretending that the prototype itself is production-ready.

**Animation:** Use short, snappy opacity and transform transitions under 240ms. Score bars reveal once on load; cards lift by 2px on hover; tabs switch immediately for keyboard navigation. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** Space Grotesk for display and labels; IBM Plex Mono for evidence, numbers, code-like metadata, and timestamps. Body copy uses Space Grotesk with compact line-height and deliberate measure.

**Brand Essence:** A candid project review for students who want a defensible portfolio outcome, not a flattering demo. Personality: forensic, direct, constructive.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are blunt but specific; CTAs sound like next actions in a lab notebook; microcopy names uncertainty instead of hiding it. Example lines: “The idea is stronger than the proof.” “Turn the demo into a defensible experiment.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A broken hexagon crossed by an audit slash and a single node, used as a compact mark rather than a default text logo.

**Signature Brand Color:** Signal Orange #F26B38.
